What is silly.io?

We're a community built around development, creativity, and making things that probably should've stayed as ideas. From games and applications to websites, art, coding, and completely questionable projects, this is a place to build, experiment, share your work, and learn from other creators. We like serious development, but we don't take ourselves too seriously. One day you're debugging a C++ project, the next you're making Tax Evasion Simulator, Court Date Simulator, or wondering why Final_Final_REAL_Final_v47 somehow became the production build.
